# Copyright & Legal Compliance Directive (/copyright)

A universal risk audit and compliance framework for e-commerce websites and web applications across all connected IDEs (Antigravity, ZCode, Cursor, VS Code, Zed, Cline).

## Seven Core Legal Lenses
1. **Intellectual Property & Trademarks**: Image licensing, trademark fair use, authentic sourcing.
2. **ADA Title III & Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA screen-reader compatibility, alt text, color contrast, keyboard navigability.
3. **Data Privacy & Cybersecurity**: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, GDPR/CCPA cookie consent, PII protection.
4. **E-Commerce Consumer Protection**: Accurate specifications, transparent pricing, FTC review rules, no recurring traps.
5. **Product Liability & Safety**: Hardware handling warnings, battery advisories, warranty claims.
6. **Anti-Spam Regulations**: TCPA & CAN-SPAM opt-in consent and unsubscribe protocols.
7. **Financial Transparency**: Clear tax/fee breakdowns and accurate payment disclosures.

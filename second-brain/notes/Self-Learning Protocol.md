# Self-Learning Protocol

The protocol for continuous observation, candidate extraction, and rule promotion across all connected workspaces and IDEs (Antigravity IDE, ZCode, VS Code, Severus Core).

## Promotion Ladder
- **T0 Observation**: In-memory session tracking.
- **T1 Candidate**: Logged to `candidates.md` when observed 3+ times with identical resolution.
- **T2 Universal Directive**: Promoted to `AGENTS.md` and rule files when stable across 2+ sessions or explicitly confirmed.
- **T3 Pillar Note**: Promoted to a dedicated Second Brain node when applicable across all projects.

## Guardrails & Fencing
- **Idempotent Fences**: All learned directives wrapped in `<!-- severus:learned:start -->` ... `<!-- severus:learned:end -->`.
- **Zero Hallucination Directive**: Never claim a promotion or test result without running empirical verification commands.
- **Zero Secrets**: Secrets, credentials, and tokens are strictly excluded from observation scope.
